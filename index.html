HOW TO PLAY

🎯

Objective

Destroy your opponent's ship! First to win 5 rounds takes the crown.

💥

Shooting

P1 uses SPACE, P2 uses ENTER. Shots have a short cooldown.

🛡️

Shield

P1 uses Q, P2 uses SHIFT+→. Blocks one hit but has a long cooldown.

⚡

Power-Ups

Collect glowing orbs: Speed, Rapid Fire, Triple Shot, Health.

🌀

Dash

P1 uses E, P2 uses /. Quick burst of speed to dodge attacks!

🏆

Win!

Reduce your opponent's HP to zero. The arena shrinks over time — nowhere to hide!
